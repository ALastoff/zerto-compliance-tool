Zerto Compliance Artifacts → Audit Control Mapping

Artifacts
- Evidence.csv: Event-level evidence for DR testing, LTR settings, and coverage.
- Summary.txt: Executive summary with scores and counts.
- Manifest.json: Chain-of-custody hashes, metadata, versions.
- ControlsMap.txt: Control mapping guidance.
- Log.txt / Transcript.txt: Audit trail of collection actions.
- Report.html / Report.pdf: Human-readable report for auditors.

Control Frameworks
- CIS Controls v8
  - 11.6 (Immutability): Evidence.csv (Cyber_Resilience rows), Summary (Immutability line)
  - 11.1/11.4 (Data Recovery): Evidence.csv (DR Testing rows), test results, Summary
  - 1.1–1.4 (Asset Inventory): Coverage counts (protected/total/unprotected), vCenter totals
- NIST SP 800-53 rev5
  - CP-4 (Contingency Plan Testing): Evidence.csv DR test rows, Summary test ratio
  - CP-9 (Backup): LTR repository presence and lock state (Evidence.csv Cyber_Resilience)
  - CM-8 (Component Inventory): Coverage vs Total; Manifest totals; vCenter totals
  - SC-28 (Protection at Rest): LTR immutability and encryption (where surfaced)
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022
  - A.5.30 (ICT readiness/BC): DR testing cadence and results
  - A.8.12 (Data deletion / retention): LTR retention lock notes (if available)
  - A.8.24 (Logging): Log.txt/Transcript.txt as audit evidence
- SOC 2 (Security/Availability)
  - CC7.2 (Monitoring): Regular testing, Evidence.csv audit trail
  - AOR (Availability): Coverage and successful test evidence; Report.html summary
- HIPAA
  - 164.308(a)(7) (Contingency): DR test evidence; Summary
  - 164.312(c)(1) (Integrity) & 164.312(b) (Audit Controls): Manifest hashes; logs/transcript

Where to look
- Protected vs total VMs: Summary.txt, Manifest.json (Totals), Evidence.csv (counts)
- Zerto version/site metadata: Summary.txt (Zerto Metadata), Manifest.json (ZertoMetadata)
- Certificate posture: Manifest.json (ZvmaCertificate), Log.txt warnings
- VPG LTR status and locking: Evidence.csv Cyber_Resilience domain rows
- DR test recency and pass/fail: Evidence.csv Availability domain rows

Notes
- If Analytics is unavailable, coverage is derived from VPGs or vCenter. CoverageSource and VCenterServer appear in Manifest.
- PDF generation requires wkhtmltopdf; otherwise rely on Report.html.
